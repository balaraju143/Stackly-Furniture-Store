Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common furniture shopping questions.

How can I check my order status?

Open My Orders and select the tracking option for an available order. Live status requires integration with your order management system.

Can I change my delivery address?

Review My Addresses. Address changes for existing orders may require assistance and depend on the delivery stage.

Where can I find my payment receipt?

Open Payments to review transaction records. Real receipts should be generated by your payment or order service.

How do I report a damaged item?

Use the connected support process and provide the relevant order number and details of the issue.

How can I update my account details?

Open My Profile to review the information currently associated with your account.
